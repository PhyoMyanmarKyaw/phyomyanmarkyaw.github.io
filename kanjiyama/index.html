INSIDE THE APP

Everything a kanji needs,
on one page

Each kanji has its stroke order, a picture to remember it by, its readings and real words, then a quiz and a review schedule to make it stick.

筆

Stroke order

An animated stroke order for every kanji, slow it down or step through it stroke by stroke.

書

Write it yourself

Trace it, then write it from memory. Every stroke is checked, and you earn a 優, 良 or 可 stamp.

絵

Memory pictures

A hand-drawn ink picture for each of the 110 kanji, morphing into the character it became.

読

Readings and words

On'yomi and kun'yomi with romaji, and example words grouped by reading, with furigana and audio.

試

JLPT-style quizzes

Meaning, 漢字読み and 表記 questions whose wrong answers are the slips the test looks for.

続

Review at the right time

Spaced repetition brings each kanji back before you forget it, with a daily streak and stats.

YOUR LANGUAGE

Learn in English or မြန်မာ

The whole app, kanji meanings included, speaks the language you choose. Made first for Myanmar learners, who usually study kanji through English.

FAQ

Questions

Is Kanjiyama free?

Yes. The app is free with ads. If you prefer to study without them, a one-time purchase removes the ads for good. It is not a subscription.

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for: your progress, streak and settings stay on your device.

Does it work offline?

Yes, studying works without an internet connection. Ads simply don't show while you're offline.

Which JLPT levels are included?

All 110 N5 kanji today, in 11 short lessons. N4 is next, then N3, N2 and N1.

Is it available on Android?

Kanjiyama is coming to Google Play soon.

How is my data handled?

Your study data stays on your phone. Ads and anonymous usage statistics are explained in the privacy policy.
