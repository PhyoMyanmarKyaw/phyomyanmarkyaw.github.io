Kanjiyama Privacy Policy

Effective September 24, 2026

In short: Kanjiyama has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your study progress and settings stay on your device. The app is free thanks to ads from Google AdMob, which collects some data to show and measure ads, as described below.

This policy explains how Kanjiyama ("the app"), a JLPT kanji app made by Phyo Myanmar Kyaw ("I", "me"), handles information. It applies to the app on iOS and Android.

Information that stays on your device

  • Your study progress: kanji you mark as learned or bookmark, quiz results and review schedule, and your daily study record (streak and statistics).
  • Your settings, such as language, theme, text size and daily reminder time.

This information is stored only on your device. I do not collect it, and it is not sent to me or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the reminders are scheduled on your device. No notification service or server of mine is involved, and you can turn reminders off in the app's Settings or in your phone's settings.

Pronunciation audio

Japanese words are read aloud with your phone's built-in text-to-speech voices. The app does not record or send any audio.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as the Advertising ID on Android and, only if you allow tracking, the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) on iOS;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location may be inferred;
  • information about your device and how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data.

How Google uses this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

The app asks ads to be suitable for general audiences.

Your choices

  • iPhone and iPad: the app asks for permission before any tracking. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Google or Privacy settings.
  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice any time under Settings → About → Privacy choices in the app.

If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized.

Selling data

I do not sell your personal information.

Children

Kanjiyama is a study app for learners of Japanese and is not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
