Destiny Teller Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

In short: Destiny Teller collects no data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers. Every reading is worked out on your device, and nothing you enter leaves it unless you choose to share a reading yourself.

This policy explains how Destiny Teller (ကောင်လေး ဗေဒင်, formerly Kglay Baydin; "the app"), a Myanmar fortune-telling app made by Phyo Myanmar Kyaw ("I", "me"), handles information.

What you enter

Readings use what you type or pick in the app: a birth date, a weekday, an age, or the first letters of a name. The app calculates the reading on your device. These details are not saved after you leave the reading, and they are never sent anywhere.

What is saved on your device

Only the language you choose for the app. It is stored on your device so the app opens in that language next time, and deleting the app deletes it.

No collection, tracking or ads

  • The app does not connect to the internet and has no servers of its own.
  • It contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising software, and does not track you across apps or websites.
  • I do not collect, sell or share any personal information.

Apple may give me overall figures, such as the number of downloads, through App Store Connect. These come from Apple, not from the app, and only include usage data from people who chose to share it with app developers in their device settings.

Sharing a reading

When you tap Share, the app passes the text of your reading to your phone's share sheet. Where it goes next is your choice, and that app or service handles it under its own privacy policy.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
